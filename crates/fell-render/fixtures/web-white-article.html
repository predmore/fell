A walk along the lake

The ferry left just after sunrise. Its first passengers watched the fog lift as it crossed the harbor.

From the landing, the new footpath follows the water to the old railway bridge. Benches face the reeds along the way.

At the bridge

The railway sign is still there. A map beside it marks the turn for the orchard and the shorter route back to town.

On Sundays a small market opens by the orchard, with bread and baskets of apples from the farms up the hill.
