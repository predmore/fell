Garlic, frost, and the seed swap

Plant your garlic before the ground freezes. Most of us put it in late last year and still got a fair crop, but the bulbs were small.

The seed swap is on Saturday the 28th at the hut. Bring labelled envelopes; the list is here.

Frost dates

Last year the first hard frost came on 4 November. This year the forecast says the 20th, which gives the late beans a little longer.

“Cover the brassicas, not the leeks. Leeks laugh at frost.”
