A walk along the lake

The ferry left just after sunrise. Its first passengers watched the fog lift as it crossed the harbor.

From the landing, the new footpath follows the water to the old railway bridge. Benches face the reeds along the way.

A notice at the gate lists the birds that stop here in autumn. The herons still gather on the far side of the channel.

The boatyard opens its doors on Saturday. Volunteers will show how they repair oars and replace the planks on a ferry.

At the bridge

The railway sign is still there. A map beside it marks the turn for the orchard and the shorter route back to town.

On Sundays a small market opens by the orchard, with bread and baskets of apples from the farms up the hill.

Anyone heading back after dusk can follow the lamps along the shore. The last ferry leaves from the east pier.

The library has printed copies of the route, including a shorter walk for anyone who wants to skip the hill.
